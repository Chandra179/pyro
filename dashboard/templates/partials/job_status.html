Once merge output started streaming over SSE that became a correctness problem, not just wasted bytes: each swap re-processed the history subtree, and the SSE extension's registerSSE() attaches an `addEventListener` per `sse-swap` element with no guard against re-registration. Two seconds in, every delta was being appended twice; four seconds in, three times. hx-preserve kept the node alive but did not stop it being re-processed. Not swapping it at all is what actually fixes it. #}

{# A run's graph-merge history, rendered inside job_status.html. Two modes, keyed on whether the job is still running: - running: subscribes to /jobs/{id}/graph-events (api/sse.py) and receives each streamed byte exactly once. `call-open` prepends a card into the list; that card's own elements then subscribe to their delta events. This replaced a 1s poll that re-rendered every call's entire accumulated content on every tick, so the response grew for the duration of the run. - finished: a plain static render of job.graph_history, with no subscriptions to leak. This is also what a page load of an already-finished job serves. A fresh connection replays from the first call, so reloading the page mid-run rebuilds the whole history rather than showing only what happens next. `sse-close="stream-close"` tears the connection down on the server's final frame, so a finished card doesn't hold an EventSource open. The container is emitted for the whole life of a running job (before merging starts, it is simply empty) — see the note in job_status.html on why it must never be swapped in later. The :has() rule in static/src/input.css keeps it hidden until the first card lands. #}
